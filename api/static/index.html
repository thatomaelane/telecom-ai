How to read the labels on every screen
Current values

Recorded telemetry for the latest hour, plus the live feed that refreshes every 2 minutes: energy, network load, customer experience, alerts.

Predicted values

Output of the forecasting models for the next hours: network load, power demand, grid-outage risk, and customer experience derived from the forecast load.

Model decision

What the AI recommends doing, where and when, with the reason, the guardrails it respected and the energy, carbon and Rand impact.

Historical (recorded) Actual recorded telemetry over a date range or the 60-day dataset - not a forecast.
How the numbers are produced
Data. A synthetic 60-day, hourly dataset for 500 South African sites (see the Dataset tab) built to mimic real telecom operating behaviour. It is not live operator telemetry.
Predictions. LightGBM models forecast data volume, PRB utilisation and site power; a separate model estimates grid-outage risk for the next 3 hours.
Detection. An Isolation Forest plus rule checks flag cyber and physical anomalies (brute-force logins, scans, traffic floods, theft).
Decisions. A rules-and-simulation orchestrator decides battery, generator, solar and grid use and low-load energy saving, guarded by signal-quality (SINR) and call-drop limits so energy savings never hurt connectivity.
Live feed. The Executive Dashboard re-reads site readings every 2 minutes. Values move around the latest recorded hour to demonstrate a live control tower; in production this would be the operator's telemetry stream.
Customer-experience score
A 0-100 score per site, built from the dataset's own customer-experience measurements:
30% call drop rate · 25% call set-up success · 20% downlink throughput · 15% latency · 10% SINR.
Good = 80 and above, Fair = 60 to 80, Poor = below 60. Network performance is labelled Healthy, Busy (PRB at 65% or SINR below 12 dB) or Congested (PRB at 85% or SINR below 8 dB), so the two can always be read side by side.
The weights are a documented design choice and can be tuned to an operator's own experience index.
